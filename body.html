Body Image

Body Image

Your body is not a problem to be solved.

Loving yourself, healing your relationship with your body, and tuning out the noise — because you are so much more than how you look.

We live in a world that profits from your insecurity. Every ad, filter, and "before and after" is designed to make you believe your natural body is somehow not enough. It is a lie, and it is a very profitable one.

Body image is how you see yourself — and more importantly, how you feel about what you see. Improving your body image isn't about changing your body. It's about changing the conversation you have with yourself about it. And that conversation starts right here.

Life Lessons

LESSON 01

What You See Isn't Always Real

Social media, magazines, and ads are heavily edited. The "ideal body" you're comparing yourself to doesn't exist in real life — not even for the person in the photo. You are comparing your reality to someone else's fiction.

LESSON 02

Your Body Does Extraordinary Things

Your body breathes without being told. It heals cuts. It carries you through every single day. When you focus on what your body can do rather than how it looks, your relationship with it transforms.

LESSON 03

Movement as Celebration, Not Punishment

Exercise should make you feel alive and joyful — not like penance for eating. Move your body in ways that feel good to you. Dance. Swim. Walk. Stretch. Choose celebration over punishment.

LESSON 04

Food Is Not the Enemy

Eating is an act of nourishment and joy. Diet culture has turned food into something to fear and control. Eating enough of what your body needs — including the foods you love — is not a failure. It's basic self-care.

LESSON 05

Clothes Are for Your Body — Not the Other Way Around

Wear what makes you feel good. Dress for the body you have now, not the body you think you should have. You deserve to feel comfortable and beautiful today.

You are allowed to be both a masterpiece and a work in progress simultaneously. Your body is not a before — it is the whole story.
— Sophia Bush (adapted)

Apply It Daily

1

Practise a daily body gratitude

Each morning, thank your body for three specific things it does for you. "Thank you for carrying me." "Thank you for healing my skin." This shifts focus from appearance to function and kindness.

2

Unfollow and replace

For every account that makes you feel worse about your body, follow one that celebrates diverse bodies, health, and joy. Your visual diet shapes your self-image.

3

Challenge the negative thought

When you have a negative thought about your body, ask: "Would I say this to my best friend?" If the answer is no, replace it with something you would say to someone you love.

4

Dress for joy today

Put on an outfit that makes you feel good right now — not one you're saving for when you "look better." Feeling good in your body starts with treating it with care today.

Affirmations for You

My body is worthy of love and care exactly as it is.
I am more than how I look.
I nourish my body because I love it.
I release the need to meet anyone else's beauty standards.
I dress for joy, not for judgement.
My worth is not measured by my size or shape.
